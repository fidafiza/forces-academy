Admission Process

How to Apply

Follow these simple steps to complete your admission process.

  1. Visit Forces Academy or contact our admission office.
  2. Collect and fill out the admission form.
  3. Attach all required documents.
  4. Submit the admission form with the fee.
  5. Receive confirmation and begin your classes.
Eligibility

Eligibility Criteria

Check the admission requirements for each academic program.

FSc Pre-Medical

Students who have passed Matric Science with Biology are eligible to apply.

FSc Pre-Engineering

Students who have completed Matric Science with good academic performance.

Matric Programs

Students who have successfully completed Middle School examinations.

Entry Test Prep

Intermediate students preparing for MDCAT, ECAT and other university entry tests.

Documents

Required Documents

Please submit the following documents during admission.

  • Student B-Form / CNIC Copy
  • Father's CNIC Copy
  • Recent Passport Size Photographs (4)
  • Previous Academic Result Card
  • Character Certificate
  • School Leaving Certificate (If Applicable)
